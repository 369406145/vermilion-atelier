# Privacy Policy for Vermilion Atelier

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

Vermilion Atelier (“the App”) is a pocket Chinese ink studio that lets you draw locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

- No accounts or registration required
- No name, email, phone number, or other personal identifiers
- No location data
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No advertising identifiers

All drawings, settings, and files you create stay **only on your device**.

## 2. Data Storage

- Your artwork is saved locally in the app’s private directory.
- When you choose to export a PNG, it is written to your device’s gallery (Pictures/Vermilion Atelier) only after your explicit action.
- We have no servers that receive your drawings or any other data.
- We cannot see, access, or recover your works.

## 3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions solely for core functionality:

- **Storage / Photos**: To save and export your drawings as PNG files to your gallery.
- These permissions are used only on your device and are never used to upload data.

## 4. Third-Party Services

- The App does **not** use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.
- It does **not** share any information with third parties because no information is collected.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, it is suitable for users of all ages.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/369406145/vermilion-atelier) or contact the developer through the repository.

---

**Summary**: Vermilion Atelier is fully local. Your drawings never leave your device, and we collect nothing.
